## Café de Olla: verified facts (from their printed menu, Oct 2026)

- Coffee sweetened with **piloncillo**, spiced with **canela (cinnamon), clavo (cloves) and anís estrella (star anise)**.
- Served in a **clay jarrito**: glazed black rim and handle over amber/terracotta glaze (real photo: `assets/cafe-de-olla-jarrito.jpg`).
- **$5.99, refills $2.99.**
- NOT verified (don't state as fact): that it's brewed in an olla de barro (clay pot), orange peel, a family recipe, the coffee's origin or brand. Mark any such line "CONFIRM WITH OWNER".

## Voice & tone

- **Tone:** warm, proud, homestyle, a little playful; a neighbor who cooks like your abuela. Plainspoken West Texas Mexican.
- **Words we use:** como en casa, hecho a mano, de olla, piloncillo, canela, jarrito, calientito, sabor de casa, en boca de todos
- **Words we never use:** cheap/barato, fast food, fusion, gourmet, artisanal (in English), corporate phrases
- **Language:** bilingual audience. Videos are currently in English (Nora). Spanish copy uses Mexican Spanish as spoken in West Texas (tú form, e.g. "Llama para ordenar") and needs a cloned native voice.
