# Hook bank: open on motion, name who it's for, promise fast

Use these as starting points, not copy. Every claim still comes from verified facts.

| Category | Motion opener (0–3 s) | Text hook pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee / drinks | slow pour into the real cup, steam bloom | "<time> and it already smells like <spice>." |
| Plates / food | sizzle on the plancha, cheese pull, salsa drizzle | "This is what <city> eats for breakfast." |
| Bakery / dessert | knife through layers, glaze drip | "Gone by noon. Every day." |
| Auto / tire / body shop | wheel spin-down, before→after panel reveal | "Your car shouldn't sound like that." |
| Home services (roofing, HVAC, plumbing) | storm-damage → fixed roof reveal, water stop | "Hail hit last night? Here's what to do first." |
| Oilfield / industrial / B2B | heavy equipment moving at dawn, crew boots | "Downtime costs more than this." |
| Retail product | hand pick-up, 360° turn, unboxing | "The <product> everyone in <city> is asking about." |
| Professional services | messy desk → clean dashboard, phone ringing → answered | "Still doing <pain> by hand?" |
| Truck parking / logistics | rig pulling into a lit, gated lot at night | "Park it. Sleep. Wake up to your rig right where you left it." |

Beat 2 ("how it comes together") ideas: ingredients one by one, process steps with
labels, feature close-ups, a before→after split, a credentials montage (licenses, trucks, crew).
Beat 3 proof options: real price, star rating + count, years in business, warranty, response time.
