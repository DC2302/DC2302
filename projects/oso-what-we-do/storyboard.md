### Register note for this video (applies to all blocks)
Same locked palette and linework as the siblings, different **time of day and energy**. This is **daylight, shop-floor, proof-of-work — foreman energy**: hard high-key light raking through shop-bay windows and roll-up doors, bright chrome, crisp shadows, dust and airborne particulate in the light. The light source changes; the palette does not. Daylight here means **cool white and ice-blue daylight**, never warm/golden/amber — that is the one thing that would read as color drift against "Sleepless Nights" (night) and "Flying Blind" (dusk). Every block bans warm cast explicitly.

Continuity across blocks: one world — the same steel-navy shop/yard with the same chrome fixtures, the same wall-mounted panel, the same trucks. Vary composition and camera, not world or palette.

## Notes for the video designer
- **Attachments per block:** style key `cd119d60-892c-42a7-9404-3144912bce87` on all four. Grizzly reference `e4c62fde-9371-47e7-80f1-9ba9dbca563d` on Blocks 1, 3, 4 (Block 1 = forearm/paw only, face out of frame).
- **Aspect:** pass `9:16` explicitly on every generation call. Per the brand profile, decline the "3D RENDER" preset with `declined_preset_id` if intercepted — this style is 2D badge illustration, and a 3D preset is the most likely source of style drift.
- **Zero baked text, zero readable signage, in every clip.** Boards, phones and panels glow with abstract shapes and bars only. If any generation returns legible letters or numbers anywhere in frame, re-roll — do not accept and crop.
- **Safe zones:** all four blocks are composed so the post text lands clear of the top 12% and bottom 20%. Block 1 holds the upper-middle open (cover line), Block 2 holds a clean center band per sub-beat (RUNS / FOUND / PAID), Block 3 holds the lower-middle open above the safe zone, Block 4 holds the centered upper-middle open for the end-card.
- **The one drift risk unique to this video is warm light.** Daylight here is cold white and ice-blue. Any amber, gold or sunset cast breaks continuity with both sibling videos — it is negated in all four blocks; check it on review.
- **Grizzly continuity check on delivery:** Blocks 1, 3, 4 must show the same chrome/silver fur value, the same steel-navy rolled sleeve, and the same shoulder mass. Compare the Block 1 forearm against the Block 3 reveal side by side before approving.
