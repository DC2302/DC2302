## Notes for the producer (not narration)
- Word counts: Block 1 = 22 words, Block 2 = 24, Block 3 = 25 (eight of them are single-digit phone numbers, which read fast). All sized for ~8-9 s at a warm, unhurried read.
- Claims used: only the verified facts (piloncillo, canela, clavo, anís estrella, jarrito de barro, $5.99, refill $2.99, address, phone, hours, 4.5 stars/39 reviews). No olla de barro brewing, family recipe, coffee origin, process timing, or "hecho a mano" for the coffee. No "barato".
- Superlative: framed as pride in the VO ("para nosotros, el mejor"), kept per the user's decision. The on-screen headline states it plainly; no ranking or award language is paired with it.
- Price and refill are on screen only (not in the VO) so Block 3's voice can carry address, hours and phone, which are the conversion actions.
- Trim option for Block 3 if the generated read runs over 9.5 s: drop "Martes a sábado desde las seis" from the VO (the full hours stay on screen).
- Pronunciation: "South Terrell" is said the way Midland locals say it. If the Spanish voice mangles it, use "Terrell" alone or spell it phonetically for the voice step. Phone is read in groups of 3-3-4.
- Brief CTA wording "de martes a sábado desde las 6 de la mañana" was shortened to "Martes a sábado desde las seis" to fit the time. The meaning is the same.
- Narrator: Soraya (locked preset voice, female, Mexican Spanish). Lines are written in tú form and as a warm invitation from home. Every line starts a new sentence on its own breath so the voice can smile through "calientito" and "como en casa".
