Note on the house "non-photoreal" rule: this client's profile explicitly specifies a cinematic PHOTOREAL food commercial (chosen by DC 2026-10-31), so the usual non-photoreal/no-realism wording is replaced below by the opposite: photoreal, live-action look, and NEGATIVE bans cartoon/illustration/CGI instead.

- **STYLE descriptor (prompt for the one style-key image):**
  "Cinematic photoreal food-commercial still, vertical 9:16, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with an 85mm macro-capable lens at f/2.0, shallow depth of field. A single small clay jarrito sits on a rustic dark reddish-brown wood table in the upper-center third of frame, a thin curl of white steam rising from the dark coffee in it, backlit by soft golden 6 a.m. window light coming from the upper left, long warm highlights on the wood grain, deep soft shadows in night #141015. THE JARRITO matches the attached reference photo exactly: a small round-bellied handled clay mug with a wide flared neck, glossy black glaze across the rim, neck and handle, honey-amber (#F2A21B-leaning) glaze on the round belly fading to raw terracotta at the base, a few small worn orange chips along the rim edge, amber glaze inside the cup. Foreground, softly out of focus at the lower-left: two cinnamon sticks, three whole cloves, one star anise, a dark piloncillo cone with a few brown sugar crystals, scattered on the wood (macro texture visible). Background bokeh: warm window glow, a hint of papel picado flags in En Boca red #CE1126 and cempasúchil gold #F2A21B, and a blurred blue-and-white talavera tile edge in cobalt #1F3F95, all unrecognizable and out of focus. Rich, cozy, appetizing grade: warm amber midtones, crushed but detailed blacks, red and gold accents only as small touches, gentle film grain, natural lens bloom around the window light. Dark empty wood in the lower 20% and right edge of the frame. No text, no logos, no people, no faces."
- **Key-image hold rules (apply to every clip):** warm 6 a.m. golden light from camera-left window; steam always present and always white-soft; dark wood table; jarrito identical in every block; accents (red/gold/cobalt) only in out-of-focus background or small props; no cool/blue daylight grade; same grade across blocks (do not drift to teal-orange or desaturated).
- **Aspect:** 9:16 (1080x1920 target).
- **Safe zones (all clips):** keep the bottom ~20% and the right ~6% edge visually calm (dark wood / soft bokeh, no key subject, no bright highlights) so captions, price stack and platform UI sit there cleanly. Primary subject lives in the upper-center band (roughly the top 15% to 75% of the frame, centered slightly left of true center).

In the soft foreground at the lower-left, out of focus, one cinnamon stick and one star anise on dark wood; hard-edged, readable steam plume against the window glow. The scroll-stopper: appetizing, glossy, instantly legible.
 (2) 3.0-6.5 s, medium macro: the full JARRITO now filled with dark coffee, steam curling up through a shaft of golden window light. Slow rack focus from the cinnamon stick and clove in the foreground to the steaming cup. A hint of blurred papel picado (red and gold) and cobalt talavera tile in the background bokeh. Leave a quiet, dark area in the upper-center of the frame, above the cup, for the claim text added in post.

Calm, confident, "this is the one" frame. No people, no hands in this block.
MOTION: Beat 1: 120 fps look, slow-motion pour, locked-off macro with a very slight 2-3% push-in. Beat 2: slow rack focus plus a gentle 3% push-in, steam drifts naturally. Beat 3: smooth, slow dolly-back / pull-out of about 8%, ending steady. No shake, no whip pans, no zoom jerks. Steam is the only fast-moving element; coffee ripples settle naturally.
AUDIO: Ambient only: soft liquid pour into clay (a warm hollow "glug", not a splash), faint steam hiss, quiet dawn room tone with one distant, soft ceramic clink. No voice, no narration, no music (VO and any music bed are mixed in post).
NEGATIVE: faces, people, any text, captions, letters, logos, watermark, olla de barro / clay cooking pot, stove, flame, saucepan, kettle, pitcher, any visible vessel the coffee is poured from, cartoon, illustration, anime, 3D render look, CGI plastic sheen, color drift to teal or cool blue, over-saturated orange, extra cups or mugs, white or glass mug, plain black mug, jarrito without black rim or without handle, jarrito that does not match the reference, saucer, whipped cream or latte art, orange peel, tall latte glass, lip-sync, motion blur smear, harsh specular blowouts, anything in the bottom 20% or right edge of the frame.
