# Creative Brief: "El Café de Olla de las 6 a.m." (En Boca de Todos, Midland)

Brand source: `brand/clients/en-boca-de-todos/brand-profile.md` (client profile; OSO profile not used).

- **Goal:** lead-generation (walk-in and phone conversion). The video drives one action: come to 703 S Terrell St (or call) for a café de olla. It is a direct-response ad, not a brand-trust piece.

- **Audience:** Midland/Odessa Permian Basin locals: Hispanic families and early-start oilfield and trade crews who drive past dozens of gas-station coffees and want a real Mexican coffee that tastes like home before a 6 a.m. shift or a weekend breakfast. Pain in one sentence: "Todo el café por aquí sabe igual, y nada sabe a casa."

- **Core promise:** En Boca de Todos serves a real café de olla (piloncillo, canela, clavo, anís estrella, in a clay jarrito) for $5.99 with $2.99 refills, and it is worth the drive to 703 S Terrell St. The positioning line the client asked for is "the best Mexican-style coffee in the Permian Basin"; it is used as an owner-approved claim, see Risks.

- **Hook (first 2 seconds):** pattern-interrupt options, strongest marked.
  1. **(STRONGEST) Sensory + time-of-day, targets the 6 a.m. crowd.** Extreme close-up: dark coffee pouring in slow motion into the black-rimmed clay jarrito, steam rising, cinnamon stick and star anise in frame. Burned-in text: **"6 a.m. y ya huele a canela."** VO (voice over), first beat: "Si madrugas… esto es para ti." Why it wins: the scroll-stopper is a macro pour with steam (instantly appetizing and silent-friendly), and the line speaks to the crew that starts early. "6 a.m." is a verified fact (Tue-Sat doors open 6 a.m.).
  2. Question hook: "¿Cuándo fue la última vez que tomaste un café que sabe a casa?" (emotional, slower).
  3. Local-challenge hook: "El café de gasolinera no es café de olla." (contrastive, riskier tone; avoid naming or attacking competitors).

- **Message arc:** (30 seconds = 3 blocks; the arc maps beat to block)
  - **Block 1 (0-10 s) HOOK + PROMISE.** Macro pour into the jarrito, steam. Line 1: the 6 a.m. hook. Line 2 states the promise: this is the café de olla people in Midland are talking about ("en boca de todos"), and the claim "el mejor café de olla de la Cuenca Pérmica" lands on screen as text (subject to owner OK). Closes on a wide of the jarrito on the counter.
  - **Block 2 (10-20 s) INGREDIENTS + HOW IT COMES TOGETHER.** Quick visual list, one ingredient per cut with on-screen label: **piloncillo** (dark cone/brown sugar blocks) -> **canela** -> **clavo** -> **anís estrella**, then all of them meeting the coffee, then the pour into the clay jarrito. VO: "Café endulzado con piloncillo, con canela, clavo y anís estrella… y servido en jarrito de barro, como en casa." This is the authenticity and "how it comes together" the client asked for, using only the verified facts.
  - **Block 3 (20-30 s) PRICE + PROOF + CTA.** Hands wrapping around the jarrito, first sip (face not required; hands and silhouettes only). On-screen price stack: **$5.99** and **"Refill $2.99."** Proof chip: **"4.5 estrellas en Google"** (from 39 reviews, Oct 2026; reviews single out the café de olla). VO CTA: "Ven por tu café de olla: 703 S Terrell St, Midland, de martes a sábado desde las 6 de la mañana. O llama al 432-218-7078." Final ~3 s is the end card (logo on a light disc, address, phone) per the client's standing instruction.

- **CTA:** Primary: **"Ven por tu café de olla: 703 S Terrell St, Midland."** Same breath, secondary route to the same action: **"Llama para ordenar: (432) 218-7078."** Hours line on screen: **"Mar-Sáb desde las 6 a.m. · Dom desde las 7 a.m. · Lunes cerrado"** (full hours, so nobody shows up on a Monday; the VO says only "de martes a sábado desde las 6"). `en-boca.com` appears as small text on the end card only. Because there is no Instagram link yet, the CTA never says "link en bio"; it relies on address + phone, which works identically on all three platforms.

- **Platforms:** Primary: **TikTok 9:16** (1080x1920). Secondary: **Instagram Reels 9:16** and **Facebook Reels 9:16** (same master file). Keep key text and the end card inside the center safe zone (roughly 150 px from the top, 270 px from the bottom, 60 px side margins) so platform UI (captions, buttons, handle) does not cover the price, address or phone. Burn in Spanish captions (most viewers watch muted). Suggested caption copy for each post, written by the later caption step: lead with the address and hours, tag Midland/Odessa/Permian.

- **Length:** **30 seconds = 3 blocks.** Why 30 and not 40-60: the reel has to carry hook, 4 ingredients, the pour, price, proof and CTA, which fits in 30 s at ~10 short cuts, and 30 s keeps completion rate high on cold audiences. Fewer blocks (20 s) would cut the ingredients, which the client explicitly asked to explain; more (40+ s) would pad a single-product ad.

- **Tone notes:** Warm, proud, homestyle, a little playful, a neighbor who cooks like your abuela. Plainspoken West Texas Mexican Spanish, **tú form** ("Ven", "Llama", "tu café"). Use brand words: como en casa, hecho a mano (only for things verified; the coffee itself is not claimed as "hecho a mano"), de olla, piloncillo, canela, jarrito, calientito, sabor de casa, en boca de todos. Never: barato/cheap (describe the price as "$5.99" and "refill a $2.99", never "barato"), fast food, fusion, gourmet. Palette cues for text and end card: rojo #CE1126, cobalto #1F3F95, cempasúchil #F2A21B, night #141015, warm paper #FBF9F8; papel picado and talavera motifs allowed as light accents. No invented owner or staff faces: hands and silhouettes only. Voice-over: one warm Mexican-Spanish narrator; the narrator voice is not yet locked in the profile, so lock it on this first run.

- **Proof points / facts used:** (all from the client profile; nothing else is used)
  - Coffee sweetened with piloncillo, spiced with canela, clavo and anís estrella. (Printed menu, Oct 2026)
  - Served in a clay jarrito with glazed black rim and handle over amber/terracotta glaze. (Printed menu; real photo `assets/cafe-de-olla-jarrito.jpg`)
  - $5.99, refills $2.99. (Printed menu)
  - 703 S Terrell St, Midland, TX 79701; (432) 218-7078; Tue-Sat 6 a.m.-2 p.m., Sun 7 a.m.-2 p.m., closed Monday; en-boca.com. (Profile)
  - Google 4.5 stars from 39 reviews (Oct 2026); reviews single out the café de olla. (Profile)
  - Brand line: "como en casa". (Profile; identity statement)

- **Risks:**
  - **Superlative claim.** "El mejor café de olla de la Cuenca Pérmica" / "best Mexican-style coffee in the Permian Basin" is the client's requested positioning but it is subjective and not backed by a ranking. Keep it as opinion/pride framing ("el que todos andan platicando"), do not pair it with statistics like "#1" or "most awarded", and get the owner's OK before publishing.
  - **Recipe-process claims are NOT verified.** The profile does not confirm brewing in an olla de barro (clay pot), orange peel, a family recipe, or the coffee's origin or brand. "How it all comes together" is therefore shown only as ingredients meeting coffee, then the pour into the jarrito; do not show or say a clay pot on a fire, hours of simmering, or "receta de la abuela" unless the owner confirms. No steps, timings or quantities are invented.
  - **Hours.** Opening is 6 a.m. Tue-Sat but 7 a.m. Sunday and closed Monday; the hook's "6 a.m." must not imply seven-day service. Hours are therefore on screen in full.
  - **Refill terms.** The menu says "refills $2.99"; whether it applies dine-in only is unknown. The line says just "Refill $2.99", no further promise.
  - **"Hecho a mano".** This brand word applies to the tortillas and guisados, not verified for the coffee; do not apply it to the café de olla.
  - **No Instagram handle/link yet.** CTA relies on address and phone; if an IG handle is created later, add it to the end card in a re-cut.
  - **AI-visual risk.** Generated clay jarrito and spices must match the real jarrito photo (black glazed rim and handle, amber/terracotta glaze); use the real photo as a reference or a literal end-frame so the product looks like the one customers receive. Avoid garbled text and invented logos on AI-generated frames; all text is burned in during post.
  - **Pricing may change.** $5.99/$2.99 are from the Oct 2026 menu; re-verify before any later re-use of the video.
