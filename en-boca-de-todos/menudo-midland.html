Menudo in Midland, every Friday to Sunday

Every weekend the pot is on at En Boca de Todos on Terrell St. Menudo and pozole are served Friday through Sunday, and caldo de res joins them Saturday and Sunday. These are slow-simmered soups, so come early: when the pot is empty, it's gone until next week.
