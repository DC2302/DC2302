Privacy policy

This website is the online home of En Boca de Todos Mexican Food, 703 S Terrell St, Midland, Texas. We don't sell anything here and there are no accounts or forms, so we collect very little.

What we collect

We use Google Analytics to count visits: which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors are (city level), what kind of device and browser they use, and how they found us. We also count taps on our phone numbers, directions links and Google reviews link, so we know how people reach us. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this. We don't collect your name, email or phone number on this site.

When you call us, the call goes through your phone company like any other call. The site remembers your language and day/night choice in your own browser; that never leaves your device.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com.
